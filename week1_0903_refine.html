1. How I Found This Work?

I started by browsing OpenProcessing , hoping to find a project that would catch my attention. I came across this sketch , which made me think of techno music. That association reminded me of a visual art exhibition I visited in Shanghai over the summer called “Loading...Access,” and a work by Raven Kwok that I wanted to revisit.

2. 1D985 by Raven Kwok

2.1 View 1D985 and watch the video

2.2 About Raven Kwok

I have always been more drawn to hyperpop than techno. To my ears, hyperpop carries a restless, futuristic energy, while techno’s repetition has felt harder to connect with. Encountering 1D985 shifted that relationship. The work seemed to give the rhythms I associate with techno a spatial form, and repetition became something I could follow with my eyes and almost inhabit. Through this meeting of sound and image, I began to feel the appeal of a musical language I had previously struggled to enter.

What stayed with me was a subtle tension between control and the possibility of losing it. I could sense an underlying order, yet each vibration, flip, and shift in space pulled my attention in a new direction. All these movements reminded me of the different paths thought can take, sometimes following a line, sometimes spiraling, sometimes expanding outward in several directions at once. The work gave these shifting states of attention a spatial presence.

As a longtime admirer of Christopher Nolan’s INTERSTELLAR , I also found myself thinking about black holes and dimensions beyond ordinary perception. These associations hovered at the edge of recognition, suggested by the work rather than clearly pictured within it. For me, 1D985 evokes the feeling of approaching an unfamiliar space whose structure can be sensed before it can be fully understood.
